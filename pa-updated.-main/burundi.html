Our Story in Burundi

Burundi is one of PA's newest ministry grounds — a small, densely populated nation in the African Great Lakes region where rural churches sit at the very heart of daily life.

PA is equipping local pastor leaders with the same holistic transformation training used across our other countries: spiritual growth, discipleship, economic productivity, youth mentoring and responsible citizenship — anchored in the local church.
