Our Story in Kenya

Kenya is the headquarters of Possibilities Africa (PA) activities. Africa's transformation journey started here in 2005, in a village called Namawanga in Webuye Constituency, Bungoma County, in the Western part of the country. Over the years, we have seen growth now spanning many villages across 32 constituencies.

We are glad to have a network of committed pastor leaders driving change. They are gradually grasping the holistic ministry model and incorporating it into their day-to-day lives at a personal level, in their churches and the community at large.
