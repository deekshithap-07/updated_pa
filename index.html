Results Areas

Five results areas that compound into lasting transformation.

Possibilities Africa stands out as a ministry appointed to bring hope and transformation across Africa through five interconnected areas of impact.

01

Transformational Leadership Development

We recruit, select, organize and equip pastor leaders to provide visionary, servant leadership for sustainable community-wide transformation.

02

Spiritual Discipleship

Guiding people in Christian faith and practice to build a solid foundation for true, eternal, and holistic community transformation.

03

Economic Productivity

Empowering rural communities to discover, develop and sustainably utilize local resources.

04

Mentoring the Next Generation

Supporting children and youth with godly values, character and practical life skills.

05

Responsible Citizenship

Churches building community programmes addressing education, water, health and infrastructure.
