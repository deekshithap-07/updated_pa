Our Work

The whole gospel, in practice.

The work of PA

The work of PA is not directed at providing one thing or fixing one problem for people. Rather, we seek the holistic transformation of the entire person and community through the whole gospel of Jesus Christ. We challenge people to see the potential that is in and around them and teach them to cultivate and utilize those resources for the betterment of their lives.

— Programs

Five transformational programs.

01

Transformational Leadership Development

Our anchor program. We recruit, select, organize and equip pastor leaders to provide the visionary, servant leadership necessary for sustainable holistic transformation.

Pastors are both beneficiaries and implementers — PA invests in developing servant, visionary, knowledgeable, godly and united leadership in their communities. Proverbs 29:18; Hosea 4:6; Nehemiah 2:17

02

Spiritual Discipleship

Guiding people in the Judeo-Christian faith and practice gives them a foundation for true and eternal transformation. We teach the whole gospel to transform the whole person and impact the whole community, equipping pastors to lead devotional discipleship and numerical church growth.

03

Economic Productivity

Rural African communities are endowed with great resources but often lack a path to use them well. PA empowers communities through pastor leader groups to see, develop and sustainably use the resources around them — an asset-based development model rather than a handouts approach.

04

Mentoring the Next Generation

Major transformational milestones unfold over a generation. We support the mentoring of children and teenagers — equipping pastors to develop programs at home, church, school and community so the next generation grows up with values, beliefs and life skills for holistic living.

05

Responsible Citizenship

We live our values as responsible citizens contributing to solving community problems. Churches build programs that impact the community outside the four walls of the church — cohesion through sports & theatre, emergency response, accountability, schools, clinics, clean water and roads.
