At PA we believe in giving what we have as a way of partnering with God in spreading the Gospel and impacting lives across Africa.

We recognize your passion and dedication to contributing to Africa’s transformation. Possibilities Africa (PA) offers various channels to make a meaningful impact by volunteering at PA’s community engagements and pastors’ efforts to transform their community, donating to our different programs, and joining PA as a staff.
